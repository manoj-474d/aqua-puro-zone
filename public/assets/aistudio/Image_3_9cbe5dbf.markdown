## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an aura of clinical purity, technical precision, and residential warmth for high-grade domestic water treatment and clean-air appliances. It bridges modern consumer electronics with certified municipal and domestic water engineering.

### Aesthetic Tone & Expression
- **Visual Stance:** Hydro-Minimalism with Subtle Glassmorphism. The aesthetic blends pure, sterile surfaces with high-clarity liquid optics, fluid-tinted ambient fills, and clean engineering geometry.
- **Atmospheric Nuance:** Surfaces mimic frosted ice, crystalline glass, and calm, mineral-rich water planes. Clean light backgrounds evoke fresh, sanitised flow rather than standard commercial software grids.
- **Emotional Impact:** The UI immediately reassures the user of health, certified water purity, swift on-site maintenance, and mechanical dependability.

### Audience
Urban and semi-urban homeowners, healthcare-conscious families, local commercial establishments, and residential builders seeking RO purification, total water softeners, whole-house sediment filtration, and kitchen exhaust systems with transparent service tracking and easy WhatsApp-based technical support.

## Colors

The palette is engineered to reflect clinical purity, dynamic fluid currents, and verified health markers.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Deep Ocean (`#0B74DE`):** Serves as the primary operational anchor, commanding structural interactive points, primary buttons, header accents, and structural trust badges.
- **Secondary Aqua Current (`#27C4D9`):** Acts as the refreshing fluid accent, used for active indicators, secondary progress bars, filter life gauges, water level tracks, and hydro-state metrics.
- **Tertiary Vital Green (`#22C55E`):** Reserved exclusively for verified certifications, 5-star customer ratings, service guarantee tokens, active warranty statuses, and high-conversion direct WhatsApp service booking actions.
- **Neutral Deep Slate (`#0F172A`):** The primary text and high-contrast structural anchor, avoiding pure lifeless black in favor of deep oceanic graphite. Secondary typographic levels leverage Slate 700 (`#334155`).
- **Canvas & Ice-Tints:** Backgrounds transition between pure white (`#FFFFFF`) and pristine mineral-wash tints (`#F0F9FF` and `#E0F2FE`), providing luminous, non-glaring contrast that accentuates purity.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the open, structural curves of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headlines with the clean, utilitarian clarity of **Inter** for dense technical data, TDS readings, warranty metrics, and operational manuals.

### Principles
- **Headlines:** Set in Plus Jakarta Sans to deliver an approachable, modern, and fluid tone while retaining precise geometry. Tight letter-spacing on display tiers gives a compact, engineered presence.
- **Body & Data Displays:** Set in Inter for maximum optical legibility across service logs, repair checklists, maintenance schedules, and filter specifications.
- **Numeric Clarity:** Numbers within TDS counters, pricing tables, and flow rate indicators should leverage tabular figure styling (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) within Inter for balanced alignment across status dashboards.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout utilizes a responsive 12-column fluid grid on desktop and tablet, collapsing to a 4-column structured grid on mobile devices.

### Breakpoints & Geometry
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `margin-mobile` (1rem / 16px), `gutter-mobile` (1rem / 16px). Service request flows, emergency call floating bars, and TDS diagnostic cards expand to full-width containers.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8 columns, 1.5rem margins, 1.25rem gutters. Product side-by-side comparisons render in a 2-column flow.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12 columns, max-width container locked to 1280px, `margin` (2.5rem / 40px), `gutter` (1.5rem / 24px).

### Spacing Rhythm
Spacing relies on a strict 8-point harmonic base. Dense data points (service logs, water chemistry specs) use `space-xs` and `space-sm` for compactness, while marketing-facing hero zones and equipment showcase stages leverage `space-xl` and higher multiples to evoke refreshing breathability and spatial calm.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses frosted glass and water-refracted light layers rather than standard opaque dropshadows.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat / Basal):** Clean `#FFFFFF` or `#F0F9FF` surfaces with subtle, crisp borders (`1px solid #E0F2FE`). Used for background page divisions and static table rows.
- **Level 1 (Submerged / Floating Cards):** Background `#FFFFFF` with an ambient cyan-tinted shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(11, 116, 222, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(39, 196, 217, 0.04)`. Outlined with `1px solid rgba(224, 242, 254, 0.8)`. Used for product specification cards, service detail panels, and review blocks.
- **Level 2 (Active Hover / Elevated Modules):** Used during cursor hover and active filter status cards: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(11, 116, 222, 0.12), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(39, 196, 217, 0.08)`. Subtle transform translation of `-2px` on the Y-axis.
- **Level 3 (Hydro-Glass Overlays & Navigation):** Semi-transparent white (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`) backed by `backdrop-filter: blur(12px) saturate(180%)`. Border rendered via `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`. Shadow: `0 8px 30px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`. Applied to sticky navigation headers, mobile booking bottom sheets, and diagnostic modal windows.

## Shapes

The shape system emphasizes ergonomic softness, echoing polished river stones and aerodynamic kitchen appliances. 

### Geometric Rules
- **Base Components (`roundedness: 2` = 0.5rem / 8px):** Applied to form text inputs, small badges, numeric table cells, and compact action controls.
- **Mid-Tier Elements (`rounded-lg` = 1rem / 16px):** Applied to primary water purifier product cards, appliance specification summaries, customer feedback modules, and modal containers.
- **Large Fluid Containers (`rounded-xl` = 1.5rem / 24px):** Utilized for hero banners, TDS measurement interactive widgets, and promotional seasonal service cards.
- **Pills (`rounded-full`):** Strictly reserved for dynamic status tokens (e.g., "In Stock", "RO+UV+UF+TDS Active"), star rating badges, WhatsApp action buttons, and filter health tags.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Deep Water Action):** Background `#0B74DE`, text `#FFFFFF`, border-radius `0.5rem`. Subtle inner highlight `inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`. On hover: `#095eb3` with shadow `0 6px 20px rgba(11, 116, 222, 0.25)`.
- **Secondary (Hydration Soft):** Background `#E0F2FE`, text `#0B74DE`, border `1px solid #BAE6FD`. Hover transitions to `#BAE6FD`.
- **Direct WhatsApp Action:** Solid `#22C55E` background, pure white text, rounded full (`pill`). Accompanied by the standard messaging glyph. Hover: `#16A34A` with glow `0 4px 16px rgba(34, 197, 94, 0.35)`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Purity Badges:** Background `#F0F9FF`, text `#0B74DE`, border `1px solid #BAE6FD`, rounded full, typographic size `label-sm`.
- **Verified / 5-Star Ratings:** Background `rgba(34, 197, 94, 0.1)`, text `#15803D`, border `1px solid rgba(34, 197, 94, 0.2)`. Displays crisp gold or green rating star indicators alongside numeric values.

### Input Fields
- White background (`#FFFFFF`), border `1.5px solid #CBD5E1`, text `#0F172A`, placeholder `#94A3B8`.
- Focus state switches border to `#27C4D9` with a subtle halo box-shadow: `0 0 0 3px rgba(39, 196, 217, 0.2)`. Error states switch border to `#EF4444`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Rounded `4px` (checkboxes) and full circle (radios). Border `2px solid #CBD5E1`.
- When checked: Background `#0B74DE`, border `#0B74DE`, crisp white check icon or concentric center dot.

### Cards
- **Product & Appliance Cards:** Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E0F2FE`, border-radius `1rem`. Houses high-resolution imagery over a soft gradient pedestal (`radial-gradient(ellipse at bottom, #E0F2FE 0%, #FFFFFF 70%)`), feature chips, pricing, and paired CTAs ("Book Demo" and "Buy Now").
- **Service Plan Cards:** Elevated border accents (`2px solid #27C4D9` for recommended plans), transparent background header with ice-blue wash, detailing periodic filter change cadences and free annual servicing visits.

### Specialized Domain Components
- **TDS Purity Level Meter:** Segmented visual bar transitioning across pure aqua (`#27C4D9`, 0–50 Optimal), balanced green (`#22C55E`, 50–150 Good), amber (`#F59E0B`, 150–300 Acceptable), to slate red (`#EF4444`, >300 Hard/Requires RO), accompanied by instant diagnostic advice.
- **Chimney Suction Gauge:** Radial or horizontal power indicator tracking m³/hr airflow output with active filter cleaning countdown monitors.
